# Eligibility, registration, and retakes

> **Verify this before preparing to book:** registration requires a Partner Network organization
> email on a recognized company domain. Personal email addresses do not work. Make this the first
> check: candidates can discover it late in their preparation.

Registration and payment go through the Anthropic Partner Academy. The Academy directs candidates to
Pearson VUE to schedule an appointment. The published fee is $125 USD; checkout reflects any
partner-tier discount that applies.

Candidates must be at least 18. They must present valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID. The name
must match the registration exactly. Send name corrections to certifications-support@anthropic.com before
scheduling.

Request accommodations through Pearson VUE, and receive approval, before scheduling:
[Pearson VUE accommodations](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/test-takers/accommodations).

Sources: [the dated exam and certification sources](../SOURCES.md), verified on 2026-09-04.

## Appointment changes

Candidates may cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before the appointment. Changes inside 24 hours
forfeit the fee. No-shows and late arrivals also forfeit the fee and require a new registration.

## Retakes and credential validity

| Result history | Earliest next attempt |
|---|---|
| First failed attempt | After 14 days |
| Second failed attempt | After 30 days |
| Third failed attempt | After 90 days |
| Any attempt history | No more than 4 attempts in a rolling 12-month period |

The credential lasts 12 months from its award date. The blueprint describes an on-time, free,
non-proctored renewal assessment on Partner Academy. A lapsed credential requires the full exam at full
fee.

## Retake math

A failed first attempt means the published $125 USD fee has already been spent and the earliest
possible second attempt is 14 days later, with another applicable exam fee at checkout. At the
published undiscounted price, four attempts would total $500 USD. If the first three attempts fail, waits
before a fourth attempt add to 134 days (14 + 30 + 90). The rolling 12-month cap allows no more than four
attempts. A passing credential then lasts 12 months from the award date.

Use those constraints to decide whether additional study would be more valuable than booking
immediately.
