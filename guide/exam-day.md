# Exam day

The exam is delivered through Pearson VUE either online with a proctor or at a test center. The exam
clock is 120 minutes, while total seat time is about 135 minutes to include check-in and the survey.

Sources: [the dated exam source](../SOURCES.md), verified on 2026-09-04.

## Online-proctored session

When testing online, remain in view of the webcam for the full session. Keep the workspace clear of
notes, books, phones, and secondary monitors. Do not communicate with anyone during the exam.

Prohibited items include mobile phones, smart watches, headphones, study materials, and recording
devices. Pearson VUE specifies permitted items, including any proctor-provided scratch paper.

Do not capture, reproduce, or share exam content in any form. Before the exam begins, each candidate
accepts a confidentiality and non-disclosure agreement that covers all content, including questions,
answer options, and scenarios. Declining the agreement ends the session without a refund.

Misconduct can invalidate a result, revoke a credential, and lead to a ban on future exams.

## Pacing

There are 53 items and 120 minutes on the exam clock. That is about 2 minutes 15 seconds per item.
Use that average as a check on pace, not as a reason to rush a difficult item.

## Appeals

Send appeals to Pearson VUE support within 14 days of notification or of the exam date.
Standard-setting outcomes and individual item content cannot be appealed.
